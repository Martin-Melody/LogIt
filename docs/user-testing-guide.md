# Testing LogIt with ~5 people in person

**Who:** five people who've never used LogIt, mostly "app-store shoppers" (persona P0 in
testing-personas.md): 2–3 who want to start training, one gym regular, one who uses Strong or Hevy.
Not people who've watched it being built.

**Setup (30–45 min each, one at a time):** their own phone, fresh install (Play internal test or APK),
no account. Android screen recorder with mic, with permission. Ideally a second person takes notes.

## What to say first
> "We're testing the app, not you. Nothing you do is wrong, and you can't hurt my feelings. Please think
> out loud: what you're looking for, what you expect, what's annoying. I won't help, because I won't be
> there when you use it for real. If you'd normally give up or delete it, just say so."

## Tasks (situations, never instructions, never the button words)
1. "You found this in the app store. Have a look around for a few minutes and decide whether you'd keep
   it." Stop at 5 min: keep or delete, and why?
2. "Get yourself set up for training this week."
3. "You're at the gym now. Do today's workout." (2–3 exercises; one set short of the reps; one weight
   with a half kilo, e.g. 62.5)
4. "You missed yesterday. What do you do today?"
5. "How much did you bench last time? Are you getting stronger?"
6. Optional: "Log what you had for lunch." / "Make sure you won't lose your data if you change phone."

## While they work
- Don't help or explain. Questions get "What would you do?"
- Stay quiet through hesitation: the pauses are the data.
- Stuck over 2 minutes: help, and mark the task failed.

Write what they did and said, not what it means ("tapped Profile looking for history", not
"navigation is confusing"). Exact quotes are gold.

## Debrief (5 min)
- How likely are you to keep using it, 1–10?
- What was confusing?
- What would make you delete it?
- One thing you'd change?
- Would you pay for this, and for what?

## Afterwards
- After each person: 10 minutes, write their top 3 problems.
- After all five: anything that tripped up 2+ people is fixed first; then by severity
  (blocked > slowed > annoyed). Send the notes to Claude to turn into fixes and tests.
- Bonus: ask them to use it for a week; check in on day 3 and day 7 ("Did you open it? Why not?").
