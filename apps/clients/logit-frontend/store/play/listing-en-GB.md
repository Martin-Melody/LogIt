## Short description (max 80)
Plans your next set from your last one. Food and habits too. Works offline.

## Full description (max 4000)
LogIt is a workout log that plans as you go. Tick off a set and it already knows
what the next one should be: the weight and reps come from your last session and
how hard it felt, so you never stand by the rack guessing.

YOUR TRAINING WEEK
• Tell it your goal, your experience and how many days you can train, and get a
  week of workouts that fits
• Or build your own split, or follow a multi-week program
• Fast set logging: weight, reps, how many more you had in you, warm-ups,
  drop sets, supersets and circuits
• Rest timer that tells you when to go again, even with your phone in your pocket
• Cardio and mobility alongside the lifting
• Control your music from the workout screen

SEE IT ADD UP
• Every session kept, with PRs and what went up
• Charts for every lift, and how your muscles are covered each week
• Goals with a date you're on pace for, and a weekly streak that you can pause
  when you're injured or away
• Insights: ready-made questions about your training and eating, answered from
  your own data

FOOD AND HABITS
• Food diary with a large food list built in, so it works without signal
• Barcode scanning and nutrition-label reading, done on your phone
• Calorie and macro targets that adapt to your weight trend
• Bodyweight log with the trend line
• Daily habits next to your training: steps, sleep, supplements, anything

AND IT'S YOURS
• The whole app works offline, with no account and no ads
• A free account backs up your phone and adds a social feed: follow friends,
  share workouts and PRs
• Export your data any time
• Make it yours with plugins: widgets, progression rules and exercise packs
• Open source: you can even run your own server

PRO AND STUDIO (OPTIONAL SUBSCRIPTIONS)
• Pro: sync between phones, the web dashboard at app.getlogit.app and deeper
  charts
• Studio, for personal trainers: invite clients, write programs, set macro
  targets and habits, read check-ins and message clients
• Being coached is free: if your coach uses LogIt, join them with their link

LogIt is a tracking and planning tool, not medical advice. Check with a qualified
professional before starting a new training or nutrition programme.
