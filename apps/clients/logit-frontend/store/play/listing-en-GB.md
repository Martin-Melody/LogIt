## Full description (max 4000)
LogIt is a training log that works the way a notebook does: open it, write your
set, done. Everything runs on your device. No account, no sign-up wall, no ads,
no tracking.

TRAINING
• Build splits and routines, or just start an empty session
• Fast set logging — weight, reps, RPE, warmups, drop sets, AMRAP, failure
• Rest timer with notifications
• Full history and per-exercise progress charts
• Progression suggestions that adapt to how your last sessions went

NUTRITION
• Food diary with a large offline food database built in
• Barcode scanning and nutrition-label reading, done on-device
• Adaptive calorie and macro targets
• Body-weight tracking with trend

HABITS
• Track daily or weekly habits alongside your training
• Simple streaks and check-offs

YOURS TO KEEP
• Works completely offline
• Export your training data to a file any time
• Open source — self-host the sync server on your own machine with one command,
  free forever
• Optional account adds cross-device sync, a social feed, and coach/client
  features — all opt-in

LogIt is a tracking and planning tool, not medical advice. Check with a qualified
professional before starting a new training or nutrition programme.
